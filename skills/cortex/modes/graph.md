# Change the graph
- `cortex_patch(id, op, target, text)` changes a note: `append_to_section` (an existing heading), `set_field` (status, outcome, score…), `check_item` (ticks a study-plan step when it is done; plans have no days or dates).
- `cortex_add(kind, title, parent?, areas?, fields?)` adds a domain, area, concept (optionally `areas=[…]` for extra parents), problem, assessment, application, mistake, resource, note, project or decision. For every kind except domain, area and concept, put a one-line `summary` inside `fields` (unknown top-level arguments are dropped silently); a domain, area or concept takes only `aliases` and `requires` in `fields`. If it answers "did you mean X?", use X unless the learner confirms it is genuinely new; only then repeat with `force=true`.
- **Playbooks (how they want a kind of task done).** When the user states or approves the steps for a recurring task ("save this as a playbook"), `cortex_add(kind="note", title=<the task>, fields={tags: ["playbook"], summary: <one line>})`; the page gets `## Steps`, `## Pitfalls`, `## Done when`; fill each with `cortex_patch append_to_section`. Only steps they stated or approved; the procedure first, pitfalls as rules ("Never …"), no stories of what went wrong. Prime serves a matching playbook; when they correct how you do that task, patch it, with `user_confirmed=true` only for their own words.
- `cortex_log(kind="assessment_result", payload={company, assessment_kind, date, outcome, score, felt_weak})` records an OA, test, mock or interview.
- **Tests need dates.** When the user mentions an OA, interview or exam with a date, add it with `cortex_add(kind="assessment", title, fields={company, kind, date, outcome: "pending", summary})`, or set the date on an existing one with `cortex_patch(id, op="set_field", target="date", text=YYYY-MM-DD)`: the exam clock ignores undated tests. Give an interview `tests` when you add it (`set_field` cannot set a map), e.g. `fields.tests={"interview": 1, "<area>": 2}`, so its stories and skills get pulled in; mocks and the story bank: `modes/mock.md`. When they say how a past one went, log `assessment_result` with its `assessment` id, `outcome` (`taken` until the result is out) and `felt_weak`.
- The learner may ask you to edit their profile or teaching contract (`me/…`). Do it with `cortex_patch`, and say exactly what you changed. A teaching-contract rule always waits for their **Approve** in the Cortex app (Review), even with `user_confirmed=true`; tell them it is waiting there.
- **About the learner (personality, preferences): save it, don't stop to ask.** Their own words ("I prefer pictures"): `cortex_patch(id="me/personality" or "me/preferences", op="append_to_section", target=<section>, text=<one line quoting them>, user_confirmed=true)`. Your inference from how they act: the same call with `user_confirmed=false`; it waits for their **Approve** in the Cortex app (Review). Either way, one line saying what you saved.
- **When a line about them changes** (lines on `me/personality|preferences|goals|profile` end in an id like `^c-7k2qd`):
  - no longer true but was: `cortex_patch(id=page, op="revise", target=<claim id>, text=<new wording>, user_confirmed=true)` (the old line goes to `## Past`);
  - simply over: `cortex_patch(id=page, op="retire", target=<claim id>)`;
  - they say a proposed line "isn't me": `cortex_patch(id=page, op="reject", target=<the proposed line>)`, and never propose it again.
- **"Sometimes, when …":** if they confirm only in some situations, save the line as `<text> — sometimes: <when>`.
- **Journal:** at natural stopping points, `cortex_log(kind="journal", payload={text})` with one line on what they did, decided or learned, linking pages as `[[id]]`. Record mood/energy (1–5) only when they state it.
- **Undo one record:** `cortex_log(kind="void", payload={event_id, reason})` when the learner says a recorded answer was wrong.
